Experiment brief

THE DECISION

Can SingleKey generate profitable activated TekSavvy customers?

Optimize for confirmed activations and paid commission—not email opens or landing-page clicks alone.

01

Eligible audience

  • Tenants who complete an application or eLease in the pilot province
  • Include serviceable addresses only; exclude prior converters and recent recipients
  • Randomize holdout, in-app, email, and combined cells if sample permits
02

Sequence

T+0Offer self-serve plans or phone support from SingleKey
48–72hOne reminder to non-clickers; suppress known activations
Day 14+Reconcile activations and commissions
03

A/B design

ASubject names the service address
BSubject names the move-in task

Change only the subject. Keep sender, preheader, body, CTA, timing and offer page constant.

04

Measurement hierarchy

  1. Primary: activated customers ÷ eligible tenants
  2. Economics: contribution per eligible tenant
  3. Incrementality: lift versus a randomized holdout
  4. Diagnostic: plan clicks, phone-link clicks, and completed orders
Before launch

Confirm TekSavvy can report activation and commission against this partner link, support placement and variant sub-IDs, and attribute orders completed with SingleKey assistance. Without that, the test measures interest rather than business conversion.
